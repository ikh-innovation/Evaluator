7. Quality and efficiency of the implementation

7.1 Work plan structure

Project CORMORANT (fictional test input) is organised into six work packages
over 36 months. WP1 "Management and coordination" (M1-M36, lead ALTAMAR)
covers project governance, quality assurance and reporting. WP2 "System
requirements and architecture" (M1-M8, lead NORDIK Dynamics) consolidates the
operational requirements of the three participating ministries of defence
into a system requirement review (SRR) at M6. WP3 "Sensor payload
development" (M6-M22, lead OPTIKON) delivers the modular inspection payload,
with a critical design review at M14. WP4 "Platform integration" (M12-M28,
lead NORDIK Dynamics) integrates the payload onto the carrier platform and
concludes with the integration readiness review at M26. WP5 "Validation
trials" (M26-M34, lead ALTAMAR) executes the two-phase trial campaign at
ALTAMAR's accredited littoral test facility in Cartagena, using the trial
protocol defined in D5.1. WP6 "Dissemination and exploitation" (M4-M36, lead
VECTIS Research) manages the exploitation board and IP registry.

The Gantt chart in Figure 7.1 shows the critical path running through
WP2 → WP3 → WP4 → WP5, with a 2-month float before the final trial window.
Inter-WP dependencies are managed through five milestones:
MS1 SRR (M6), MS2 CDR (M14), MS3 first payload prototype (M20),
MS4 integration readiness (M26), MS5 final trial report (M34).

7.2 Deliverables and milestones

Each WP produces between two and four deliverables; 17 in total. Key
deliverables: D2.2 system architecture document (M8), D3.3 qualified sensor
payload (M22), D4.2 integrated demonstrator (M26), D5.2 trial data package
(M33), D5.3 final validation report (M34). Milestone acceptance criteria are
defined quantitatively in Table 7.2 (e.g. MS3 requires detection probability
≥ 90% on the reference target set in laboratory conditions).

7.3 Management structure and procedures

The consortium is led by ALTAMAR as coordinator, supported by a project
management office staffed at 0.8 FTE. The General Assembly (one vote per
partner) decides on budget shifts above EUR 100 000; the Executive Board
(WP leads, monthly videoconference, quarterly in person) manages day-to-day
execution. A Security Advisory Board with representatives of the three
supporting ministries reviews all classified deliverables. Conflict
resolution follows a three-step escalation defined in the consortium
agreement, from WP level to General Assembly arbitration within 30 days.
Quality procedures include peer review of every deliverable by a partner not
involved in its production, and a document management system with controlled
versions operated by the PMO.

7.4 Risk management

Table 7.4 lists twelve technical, organisational and supply risks, each with
probability, impact, owner and mitigation. The three highest-rated risks are:
R2 "sensor performance below MS3 threshold in turbid conditions"
(probability medium, impact high; mitigation: parallel evaluation of two
sensing modalities until CDR, decision gate at M14, owner OPTIKON);
R5 "delay in export licence for the carrier platform transfer"
(probability medium, impact medium; mitigation: licence application submitted
at M3, fallback integration site at NORDIK Dynamics' Aalborg facility, owner
NORDIK Dynamics); and R9 "key staff turnover at SME partners" (probability
low, impact high; mitigation: cross-training plan and documented handover
procedures audited by the PMO every six months, owner ALTAMAR). Risk review
is a standing agenda item of the monthly Executive Board and the register is
re-baselined at every milestone.

7.5 Allocation of work and resources

Total effort is 412 person-months distributed as: ALTAMAR 96 PM (23%),
NORDIK Dynamics 88 PM (21%), OPTIKON 76 PM (18%), VECTIS Research 52 PM
(13%), and the three remaining SME partners 100 PM (25%) concentrated in
WP3 and WP5 where their niche expertise (acoustic materials, embedded
cryptography, trial instrumentation) is required. Effort per WP and per
partner is given in Table 7.5; no partner carries effort in a WP without a
named task, and each task lists a responsible partner and a completion
criterion. The budget allocation follows the effort distribution, with
equipment concentrated in WP3 (payload components, EUR 640 000, itemised in
Table 7.6) and trial costs in WP5 (vessel time and range fees, EUR 210 000,
quoted from the facility's published price list).
