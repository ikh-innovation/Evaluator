7. Quality and efficiency of the implementation

The project (fictional test input) will be implemented by the consortium
over a period of time appropriate to the ambition of the work. The partners
will collaborate closely and share responsibilities in a flexible manner,
adapting the organisation of the work as the project evolves. Work will be
divided into work packages covering management, research, development and
testing, which will be defined in detail after the project starts.

The coordinator will ensure smooth communication between the partners
through regular meetings. Decisions will be taken by consensus whenever
possible. The consortium believes that its long experience guarantees
efficient management without the need for heavy formal procedures.

Risks are not expected to be significant given the maturity of the
underlying technologies. Should unexpected problems arise, the consortium
will address them promptly and reallocate resources as needed. The partners
are confident that the objectives will be reached within the planned budget.

The allocation of effort between partners will be balanced and fair,
reflecting each partner's capabilities and interests. The exact distribution
of person-months will be refined during the negotiation phase. All partners
will contribute to all work packages to maximise collaboration and knowledge
exchange across the consortium.

Deliverables will include reports and demonstrations at appropriate points
in the project lifetime. A detailed deliverable list and timetable will be
produced as one of the first tasks of the project.
